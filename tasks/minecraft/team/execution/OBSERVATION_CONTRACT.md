## Avoid unknown-state deadlock

Fresh self air is needed to claim an oxygen threshold/trend. It is not required to acknowledge a current dry camp body, route refusal, or successful observed return. If no self-air packets arrive in dry camp, record `air unknown (no current-life receipt)` and proceed only with a small owner-chosen test whose dry escape does not depend on that unknown reading. Use a strict wall/work budget, direct known shallow land exit, and ample retained reserve; do not extend immersion waiting for a packet. Abort the *qualification claim* if evidence stays absent; report actual motion/return separately. No missing update is itself a reason to assert emergency or recovered air.
