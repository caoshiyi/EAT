* **Verify the actual planner start.** #10408 found a complete113-node path from a hypothetical Y26 cell, then showed its floorY25 was AIR. Actual egress was unqualified. A successful model starting in unsupported air is not an escape. Check source feet, support, headroom and intended opening before pricing travel.
* **Keep negatives bounded.** H1/H2 completed noPath results apply to loaded terrain, settings and the stated start. They do not prove global inaccessibility. H7 local timeouts are not noPath. A failed delivery before an experiment is an operational failure, not a negative mechanism result.
* **Test cost settings on a real small branch.** My `digCost=100` made planned cuts exceed the movement rejection threshold; #10227 did not test hand-cut feasibility. Corrected #10231 allowed cuts. A zero-length current-goal smoke does not exercise exclusion/dig branches: #10356's `return100` typo survived parsing and failed on the first nontrivial field.
* **Do not blame optimization without a paired witness.** #10358 continued17 partial yields: optimized/unoptimized both returned46-node success with no feet in full blocks. This does not explain P1's earlier path/terrain mismatch or justify disabling optimization globally.
* **Static “empty” is not a complete interaction contract.** H4 loose17/17 versus strictAIR11/17 differed at thin SNOW over SAND. Empty collision geometry does not establish legal target selection, replacement, supported gravity, entry or same-caller closure. Current camera/reach checks remain mandatory.
