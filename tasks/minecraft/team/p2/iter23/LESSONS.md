# ITER23 retained execution lessons

These are bounded observed cases, not safety guarantees or Minecraft-impossibility claims.

- **A returned dig is not carried material.** 10420 normal `dig(block,false,'raycast')` returned at1460ms; a real item appeared1661ms; ordinary GRAVEL+1 arrived2161ms. Reclosure returned31->30. P1's adapted10424 saw a real BASALT drop/stableAIR but no pickup. API choice/timing both changed; no isolated cause claim. Use stable target state + a new actual drop or inventory delta to qualify a cut; use ordinary inventory ONLY to price carried caps.
- **Qualify passage after the cuts.** 10428 two real receipts, one-cell movement, twenty settle ticks and return/reclosure passed with no forcedMove event. This does not prove a long route. P1's jointly changed10427 WEST operation succeeded after two failed fields; exact method included new cut witnesses and settled normal movement.
- **Waypoint support does not cover the swept body.** 10432 reached upper native nodes then fell across the known missing west floor during a diagonal into the old room. 10434 two supported gravel pillars, cardinal entry and full closure worked. Validate continuous support/actual footprint at transitions, not just destination-floor truth.
- **A hypothetical model start may itself be occupied or floating.** 10400 outside-cell AIR admission refused before walking; P4's far model omitted changed egress floors. Completed modeled paths are navigation candidates. 10337 explicitly distinguished empty SUCCESS from empty noPath; preserve start/goal/status/partial sequence and never infer traversal from a returned path.
- **Error text may describe an earlier phase.** 10269 printed the quarry failure after a successful reverse/closure. 10409 had an unexplained position discontinuity without death; its failed reverse assumed the old body. Log immutable target/time/phase, release+settle body/velocity and documented forcedMove; do not invent a server/physics cause.
- **A guard stop must end in actual protection.** 10440's failed material gate left the body atY24 while its only fallback admittedY26; Zombie killed it. Later preflight refusal left the new hill body exposed while I analyzed; Skeleton killed it. These were avoidable operator failures. Prepare a current-state feasible ending in the caller; distinguish forward admission from emergency feasibility. After any error/death/transition reconcile real body and act before reporting.
- **Full ending evidence must be after the last mutation.** 10460 normal cuts/steps/caps gave four fresh ten-full samples; 10469 rechecked ~8min later. Floor and north head changed DIRТ->GRASS while staying full. Fresh material/pose/eye/support matters even without player edits; old success flags and old material whitelists are dated.
- **Cap budgets require whole receipt-to-use cost.** Old quarry partial30GRAVEL was real, but no surface/food/recipient use resulted. 16:10 stock,16:44 surface,16:48 transfer,16:50 food,17:06 surface and17:15 food-use gates all failed. Failed clocks remain failed; changing a method can justify a new bounded test, not relabel earlier partials as completion. Never let resource arithmetic assume unreceived drops.
- **Centralized recovery still needs execution.** P1 retained a shared tool and real Nether access; P2/P4 were not required to reproduce that kit. P2's attempted food support consumed time and two bodies without adoption. Keep a concrete recipient/ordinary receipt/actual use contract, accept useful partials truthfully, and retire duplicate or stalled source chains.

Permitted-source boundary: no void17–19 methods or internet-derived mapping/bytecode claims adopted. Ordinary actual-target dragon interaction remains untested this iteration; zero attacks never proves immunity. Start current HP/action observations before any future lawful interaction, not only after return.
